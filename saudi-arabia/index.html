Kenyan meat for Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is one of the main buyers of Kenyan goat and sheep meat. We supply whole chilled carcasses to importers, wholesalers and butchery groups, slaughtered at SFDA-approved Kenyan export abattoirs and flown from Nairobi to Jeddah in about four hours and to Riyadh in about five.

  • 14.5%of Kenya's goat and sheep meat exports went to Saudi Arabia in 2023
  • ≈1,400 tof Kenyan goat meat bought by Saudi Arabia in 2021
  • 4 hNairobi to Jeddah by air

Flights from Nairobi

  • JEDJeddah, about 4 h
  • RUHRiyadh, about 5 h
  • SFDA rulesMeat must come from an establishment approved by the Saudi Food and Drug Authority, with a halal certificate from an SFDA-accredited body. Every shipment carries both, with the veterinary health certificate and certificate of origin.
  • SeasonDemand peaks in Ramadan and the Hajj season. Book weekly programmes for those weeks early, because flight space tightens.
  • What sellsWhole Galla goat carcasses of 7–12 kg and sheep carcasses of about 15 kg, wrapped in white cloth and labelled in Arabic.
  • PricePer kg of carcass weight, FOB Nairobi (JKIA). Air freight to your airport on a separate line at the day's rate.
  • MinimumFrom about 1 MT per shipment for a trial; weekly programmes from 5 MT.
  • Lead timeUsually 7–10 days from deposit to landing, depending on flight space.
  • PaymentDeposit on confirmation and balance before the air waybill is released, or a confirmed letter of credit.
  • DocumentsHalal certificate, veterinary health certificate, certificate of origin, invoice, packing list and air waybill.
