Bulk supply FAQ

What's the minimum order for free delivery?

Bulk orders ride free across Nairobi and environs. Smaller trial orders are welcome — we'll arrange delivery at cost. Message us your location and volume and we'll confirm your route.

Do you offer credit or invoice terms?

New accounts start on M-Pesa or cash-on-delivery. Standing accounts with consistent volumes can move to invoiced supply after a quick approval.

Is your chicken halal?

Yes — 100% halal slaughter and handling on every line, with certification available for institutional clients.

Can you cut and pack to our spec?

Yes. Send your spec sheet — portions, weights, packing, labelling, even marinades — and every delivery arrives kitchen-ready.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Daily routes cover the CBD, Westlands, Kilimani, Lavington, Karen, Parklands, Industrial Area, South B/C, Eastleigh and environs — timed before prep hours.
