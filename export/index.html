How we export

Your order, step by step.

Kenyan carcasses fly. From slaughter to your cold room is a matter of days, not weeks, which is why we ship chilled rather than frozen.

  1. EnquiryTell us the species, carcass weight, quantity and destination airport.
  2. QuotationPriced per kg, FOB Nairobi (JKIA) or with air freight on a separate line.
  3. Order and depositOrder confirmed; slaughter date fixed to the flight.
  4. Halal slaughterAt an SFDA-approved Kenyan export abattoir, veterinary-inspected.
  5. Chilling and packingChilled to about 1 °C, wrapped in white cloth, weighed and labelled.
  6. FlightExport clearance at JKIA, loaded in cold chain, documents sent before landing.

Terms

How we price and sell.

  • PricingPer kg, FOB Nairobi (JKIA). Air freight quoted separately at the day's rate, or booked in the buyer's name.
  • Minimum orderAir freight is booked per shipment; we quote from about 1 MT, and regular weekly programmes from 5 MT.

  • InsuranceCargo insurance to the destination airport arranged on request.
FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Do you ship frozen meat by sea?

Our standard is chilled carcasses by air. Frozen product by sea container can be arranged for larger programmes; ask for a quotation.

Which countries can you export to?

Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman and other markets that accept Kenyan meat with SFDA/GSO-style certification. Tell us the destination and we will confirm the documents.

What is the minimum order?

Air freight is booked per shipment. We quote from about 1 MT for a trial and from 5 MT per week for regular supply.

How long from order to delivery?

Typically 7–10 days from a confirmed order and deposit to landing, depending on flight space; the carcass itself is 2–3 days old on arrival.

Can you cut and pack instead of whole carcasses?

Whole carcasses and beef sides are standard. Six-way cuts and vacuum-packed primals can be quoted on request.
