Also called the Borana or Somali goat: a tall, long-legged white goat raised by pastoral communities across Kenya's arid north. Hardy and heat-tolerant, it thrives where few breeds can. Kenya is the world's second-largest goat meat exporter, and the UAE and Saudi Arabia are its main buyers.

In 2021 the UAE took about 7,240 t of Kenyan goat meat and Saudi Arabia about 1,400 t.

  • ProductWhole chilled carcass, halal, wrapped in white cloth, weighed and labelled
  • Raised inIsiolo, Garissa, Tana River and the northern rangelands
  • ShippingChilled, held at about 1 °C in transit; flown from Nairobi (JKIA)
  • SeasonSupplied all year; Gulf demand peaks in Ramadan and the Hajj season
  • FatGoat carries less carcass fat than lamb, for a lean, firm meat
  • Live weightMature bucks up to 70 kg, does up to 55 kg

Sources: Nation (Galla goat); DAGRIS; JKUAT; Langston University goat handbook; World Bank WITS (goat meat exports 2021, 2023). Carcass range: Beefy supply.
