حلال

Developed on Kenyan ranches since the 1920s from the zebu cattle of the Borana people: white or grey, with a black muzzle. Steers raised on grass are ready at 36–42 months, at 420–460 kg. In US trials Boran beef proved more tender than Brahman, the other heat-adapted zebu breed.

Carcasses and half carcasses make up 81% of Kenya's chilled beef exports, which go mainly to Kuwait and the UAE.

  • ProductChilled carcasses and sides, halal, weighed and labelled
  • Raised inKenyan ranches, including Laikipia and Nanyuki
  • FinishingGrass-raised steers at 36–42 months, 420–460 kg live
  • ShippingChilled, held at about 1 °C in transit; flown from Nairobi (JKIA)
  • SeasonSupplied all year
  • TendernessUSMARC shear force: Brahman 14.3 · Boran 12.4 · Tuli 10.7 · Angus 8.8

Sources: ILRI (Boran); Boran Cattle Breeders' Society of Kenya; Gatsby Africa feedlot mapping; USDA ARS USMARC Germplasm Evaluation; TrendEconomy (HS 0201, 2023).
