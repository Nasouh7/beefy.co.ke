Dorper, a meat breed from South Africa (Blackhead Persian × Dorset Horn), is hornless and barrel-shaped, with a black or white head, now raised on Kenyan ranches. In a Kenyan trial at ILRI's Kapiti ranch it was the leanest of four breed groups and earned the best carcass grade. Red Maasai is Kenya's red, short fat-tailed sheep of the Maasai rangelands, bred by nature for drought and disease: under heavy worm challenge 28% died, against 66% of Dorpers.

Trial values are from ILRI's Kapiti ranch, Kenya (live weight 36–38 kg). Carcass weight is supplied to your order.

  • ProductWhole chilled carcass, halal, wrapped in white cloth, weighed and labelled
  • Dorper raised inRanches in Laikipia, Machakos and Naivasha
  • Red Maasai raised inMaasai rangelands of southern Kenya, centred on Kajiado
  • Fat coverDorper 1.81 of 3 (lean) · Red Maasai 2.12 of 3
  • ShippingChilled, held at about 1 °C in transit; flown from Nairobi (JKIA)
  • SeasonSupplied all year; Gulf demand peaks in Ramadan and the Hajj season

Sources: ILRI Animal Genetics Training Resource (Dorper, Red Maasai); PMC5203845 (Kapiti ranch carcass trial, Kenya); The Standard (Laikipia ranchers).
