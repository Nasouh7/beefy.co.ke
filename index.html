02Why Kenyan meat

Close to the Gulf, raised on open range.

About four hours by air to Jeddah and five to Dubai, so Kenyan carcasses reach the Gulf chilled, not frozen. Australia, the largest goat meat exporter, ships almost all of its goat meat frozen. Animals from Kenya's arid rangelands are naturally fed, with lower fat, and halal slaughter is standard in Kenya's export abattoirs.

  • No. 2world's second-largest goat meat exporter (2023)
  • $145MKenya's meat exports in 2024, up from $65M in 2020
  • 68.9%of Kenya's meat exports go to the GCC
  • 70%+of Kenya's meat comes from pastoral herds
03How we work

From our cold room to your city, by air.

  1. EnquiryTell us the species, carcass weight, quantity and destination airport.
  2. QuotationPriced per kg, FOB Nairobi (JKIA) or with air freight on a separate line.
  3. Order and depositOrder confirmed; slaughter date fixed to the flight.
  4. Halal slaughterAt an SFDA-approved Kenyan export abattoir, veterinary-inspected.
  5. Chilling and packingChilled to about 1 °C, wrapped in white cloth, weighed and labelled.
  6. FlightExport clearance at JKIA, loaded in cold chain, documents sent before landing.
